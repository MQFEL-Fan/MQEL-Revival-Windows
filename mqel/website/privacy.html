Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

By installing or using the game client, the launcher, or this server, you agree to this privacy policy. If you don't agree, don't play on this server.

Mighty Quest Rebirth is a non-commercial fan server run by one person on a private computer. It is not affiliated with Ubisoft. To run the game, the server stores the data below.

Your account

  • Your login name and display (castle) name.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted one-way hash (it can't be read back).
  • Your Steam ID, if you log in with Steam or link Steam.

Connection data

  • The IP address of every login and game request, with the time. It is used to keep the server safe, to limit connections, and to detect several accounts made by one person (one account per player unless an admin allows more).
  • Your country, worked out from your IP address, shown as a flag next to your name.
  • Whether you are online, shown to your friends.

What you do in the game

  • Your save game: heroes, items, castle, currencies, quests, achievements and play time.
  • Attacks and defenses, battle logs and replays of attacks on player castles.
  • Chat messages (kept for moderation), friends, clans and parties.
  • Ingame Market offers and trades, Bling shop purchases (in-game currency only, no real money) and a record of changes to your currencies.
  • Technical logs of game requests, error reports and anti-cheat checks.

Discord

  • If the Discord app runs on your computer, the game shows your activity there (for example "Attacking a castle"). This goes straight from your game to your own Discord app.
  • Announcements on our Discord server show only patch notes and events, no player data.

Who sees it

  • The server admin, for running and moderating the server.
  • Other players see what the game normally shows: your name, castle, country flag, online status, trophies, clan, chat messages and Ingame Market offers.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Data is kept as long as the server runs. There is no option to delete your account or your data. Logs may be cleaned up from time to time.

Questions

Ask the admin on our Discord server. This policy can change; the date at the top shows the latest version. Playing after a change means you agree to the new version.
