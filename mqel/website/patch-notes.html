2026-11-24

Ingame Market: Buy now fits again

Buy now button. In Weapons and Equipment, the bid box, Bid and Buy now buttons now sit on their own line at the bottom of each item, so Buy now no longer covers the item below it. Prices keep their size.

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.5.0.3).

2026-11-23

Ingame Market and Setting Mod buttons

Ingame Market button. The button is now called INGAME MARKET, is bigger and sits on its own in the top bar, right before your heroes.

Setting Mod button. SETTING MOD now has the whole spot next to the settings gear to itself, bigger and easier to hit.

Windows on top. The item info box and the Ingame Market, party and Setting Mod windows now always open above the building shortcuts under the top bar, so nothing covers them anymore.

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.5.0.2).

2026-11-23

Orb numbers in attacks and a Sort button you can see

Orb numbers everywhere. Your health and mana numbers now also show in the orbs while you attack a castle, not only in the lobby.

Inside the orb. The numbers now fit inside the orbs and are centred: your current health or mana on top, your maximum below it.

Sort button. The SORT button now shows under your inventory, at the left of "Drag here to sell items", for every inventory tab from 1 to 8. Pick a sort, pick it again to reverse it.

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.5.0.1).

2026-11-23

Orb numbers, inventory sort and a better Ingame Market

Health and mana in the orbs. Your health and mana orbs now show how much you have left and your maximum (for example 7143 / 7143) in white, right on the orb, in the lobby and during attacks.

Sort your inventory. A new SORT button under your inventory sorts the tab you are looking at: by type, quality, level or name. Pick the same sort again to reverse it. Works on every inventory tab you have unlocked; the bag reloads in a moment with everything in order.

Ingame Market: item info. Hover over any item in any Ingame Market tab to see its full details, like in your inventory: quality, type, level and every bonus with what it does. The box always opens to the left of your mouse.

Ingame Market: two columns. Weapons and Equipment now list items in two columns, so you see twice as many at once.

Ingame Market: sort both ways. Pick the same sort again to reverse it: ending soon or last, newest or oldest, price low or high, level high or low.

Ingame Market: colours and pages. Item icons are filled with their quality colour like in your inventory, and My offers now has pages instead of a long scroll list.

Game icon. The game shows its logo in the taskbar again instead of a blank page.

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.5).

2026-11-23

Setting Mod and a loot filter for your hero

Setting Mod. The KEYS button is now called SETTING MOD. It sits right under MARKET in the top bar, so the bar is tidier and nothing overlaps. Inside you still find WASD movement and all your key binds, plus new settings.

Loot filter for your hero. Open SETTING MOD and, under "Loot pick-up by Player", tick "Apply the pet's loot filter to my hero too". Your choice under Settings > Gameplay > Loot pick-up by Pet then counts for your hero as well: with "Magic" and up, for example, plain (Basic) equipment and weapons stay on the ground for your pet and your hero. Gold, life force, blings, crafting materials, potions and orbs are always picked up. It works right away, no restart needed.

Party button. The party button now matches its neighbours, on the same dark-blue tile as Friends, Clans and Notifications.

Steam login. One launcher start logs you in with Steam again, even when your last login had expired (v1.4.4.1).

Party window and Linux. If the party window can't reach the server, it now tells you why instead of loading forever. On Linux and Steam Deck, the Ingame Market and party windows no longer mistake another program on your PC for the game server (v1.4.4.1).

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.4.5).

2026-11-23

Back home at start, roomier region map, WASD optional

Back home. The game now starts in your own region again instead of the Co-op Raids, and the Attack button takes you back to the last region you were in.

Co-op Raids sky. The Co-op Raids now have Little Spitaly's full sky all around you, and Splendorado's golden clouds are back. The Co-op Raids emblem on the region banner is now the right size.

Attack button. Fixed the crash on the Attack button that came with the previous update.

Region map. Upper Bestmount, Splendorado and Stabbinshire are spread out a little, so they no longer crowd each other.

WASD is optional. New players keep the game's own keys: the potion stays on Q and the camera turns with W and E. Want to move with WASD? Open KEYS, tick "Move the hero with the keyboard" and restart the game once. If you already use WASD, nothing changes for you.

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.4.4).

2026-11-23

Party raids: pick, ready, start together

Party raids. The party leader now picks the raid in the party window, everyone gets a Ready prompt, and when all are ready the whole party starts the same castle at the same moment. After the raid, the leader picks the next one. The party window shows which raid your friends are in.

Same castle for everyone. Once a raid is picked, it stays the same for the whole party until everyone has finished, even if someone finishes first or asks for more castles.

Invitations. Party invitations now pop up in the lobby with Join and No thanks.

Co-op Raids sky. The Co-op Raids now have Little Spitaly's sky, and Splendorado's sky surrounds you again instead of sitting below you.

Co-op Raids banner. The region banner shows the Co-op Raids emblem and levels 5-60.

Back home. The game no longer opens the Co-op Raids after a reload or at start.

Live party panel. Your friends' gold and life force now count up live during a raid.

Ingame Market. Item colours now match the game exactly: grey Basic, green Magic, yellow Exceptional, orange Legendary, purple Epic. Listing an item no longer opens the battle log.

Infinite Pack rings. The Infinite XP Ring now gives +26% XP and the Infinite Material Ring a 12.5% chance for bonus materials, a quarter of the old pay-to-win values. The XP bonus of your gear now really counts on the server.

Hero corpses. A collected hero corpse no longer leaves its marker behind.

Server updates. After an update you can log straight back in with Steam, no second launcher start needed.

Warning. The Co-op Raids warning no longer shows when you are in a party, and has a "Don't show again" box.

Restart the launcher once to get the update (v1.4.3).

2026-11-22

Raid bosses, Ingame Market collect and the version number

Raid bosses. Co-op Raids now has castles at many different levels (5, 7, 9, 10, 12 and up to 59) with real castle names, and four bosses: Warlord's Outpost (15), Warlord's Fortress (30), Warlord's Citadel (45) and Warlord's Keep (60).

New raids on demand. The "More castles" button now works in Co-op Raids (and Splendorado): it gives you, and your whole party, brand new procedural castles.

Better raid castles. Traps stay where they belong: no more lonely rail traps or trap generators without traps. The region's banner shows its boss picture.

Ingame Market. Collect one item at a time or everything at once; your bag now refreshes right after collecting. Gold from sales can go above your storage limit (upgrades still stop at your storage). Drag an item from your inventory onto the Ingame Market to sell it, and item colours now match your inventory.

Esc key. Esc closes the Ingame Market, Bling shop and party windows. With nothing open, it opens the game menu as usual.

Version number. The game menu (Esc) now shows the server's version on the Credits button. This update is v1.4.2.

Party button. The party button sits next to Notifications now, so the notifications list points to the right button again.

Restart the launcher once to get the update.

2026-11-22

Co-op Raids update, one account per player and our privacy policy

More raid castles. Co-op Raids now has 36 raid castles, three for every level from 5 to 60, plus a boss castle: Warlord's Keep.

A sky for the raids. The Co-op Raids castle selection no longer shows a black sky: it has Little Spitaly's sky now. Splendorado's sky also sits around you again instead of below you.

Fair raids. Raids no longer get more monsters for a bigger party: everyone faces the normal castle. The party bonus still grows with your party.

Know before you go. The Co-op Raids island now says it is procedural, and asks before you enter.

Map and party button. The islands on the map moved a little to make room, and the party button now looks like the other buttons in the top bar.

Ingame Market. Fixed the item picture in the market details, and the window now reconnects by itself after a server update.

One account per player. New accounts can no longer be made from an internet connection that already has an account. If you really need a second one, ask an admin on Discord.

Privacy policy. Our website now has a privacy policy (link at the bottom of every page and in the launcher). By playing on the server you agree to it.

Restart the launcher once to get the update.

2026-11-22

Parties and Co-op Raids

Parties. Team up with up to 3 friends: press the new purple Party button next to Clans, invite friends and accept invitations. The leader can remove players; anyone can leave.

Co-op Raids. A new purple island on the map. Raid castles at levels 5, 10, 15 and up to 60, built new for every party: everyone in your party gets the same castle, with more monsters the bigger your party is. Each of you plays it in your own game and keeps your own loot. The "More castles" button gives your whole party new raids.

Live party panel. While you raid, a panel on the left shows how your friends are doing: their health, gold, XP and loot, and when they go down.

Party bonus. Clear the same raid as your whole party and everyone gets extra gold, life force and crafting materials.

Decorations. Decorations now really drop their gold, life force and Bling Crystal blings.

Updates. When a server update starts, the game now closes properly at the end of the countdown.

Restart the launcher once to get the update.

2026-11-22

Ingame Market fixes, more decoration loot, chat and clans

Ingame Market. Your purchases, the money from your sales and your refunds now wait in the Collect tab until you pick them up. The Sell tab sorts your items: weapons, then equipment by class and level, then materials. You can no longer buy or bid on your own offers. Items you put up for sale leave your bag, and a listed item can't be sold to a vendor by mistake in the moment before your bag catches up.

More decoration loot. Smashing a decoration now drops a little gold or life force three times in four, always less than a monster of that castle gives. The green Bling Crystals keep their 1% chance to drop 1 bling, up to 2 per attack, and drop nothing else.

Chat. Fixed players who couldn't send chat messages after a server update: pressing Enter did nothing.

Clans. Clans set to accept join requests now let players join right away, like open clans (asking the leader for approval isn't available yet). The other members now get the join message too.

Restart the launcher once to get the update.

2026-11-22

The Ingame Market opens, and decorations drop loot

Ingame Market. Trade with other players right in the game: press MARKET in the top bar, next to KEYS. It unlocks once you finish the castle crafting tutorial, at any hero level.

Weapons and Equipment are sold by auction: bid, or use the buy-now price when the seller sets one. If someone outbids you, you get your money back. Filter by class, by gold or blings, sort and search.

Items (crafting materials and dyes) work like a market: the cheapest offer sells first, and you can place a buy order at your own price and wait for a seller.

Sell for gold or blings, your choice. A 1% fee is taken when something sells. What you buy, the money from your sales and your refunds wait in the Collect tab until you pick them up.

Decorations drop loot. Smashing decorations during an attack now drops a little gold or life force about one time in three, and once in a while a crafting material. The green Bling Crystals have a 1% chance to drop 1 bling, up to 2 per attack (part of the weekly bling loot limit).

Bling shop inside the game. The green "+" next to your blings opens the shop in a window in the game, with an X to close it. No more Steam overlay.

Update countdown. Before a server update, a timer at the bottom right shows when the game will close, in your own time. Finish your fight and log off before it reaches 0.

Restart the launcher once to get the update.

2026-11-22

Heroes pack, friends online and events all year

The All Heroes Pack in the bling shop unlocks every hero you don't own yet for 30 % less (three heroes: 2,000 blings instead of 2,850). Your friend list shows a green dot on friends who are playing and a red one on the others. New players get their real country flag instead of Germany's. Events all year: a boost weekend every month (gold, life force, XP, magic find, bling mine and material mines take turns, everything x2 in December), More Materials from July 1 to September 25, a free hero every month, plus Halloween, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day and Easter. Competitions load again.

2026-11-21

Discord status and a new launcher look

Discord now shows what you're doing in the game: attacking a castle (with its name), editing your castle, shopping, gearing up your hero or choosing your next target, with a timer and a button to join our server. It works on Windows and, with Proton 7 or newer, on Linux and Steam Deck; turn it off with DiscordPresence=0 in GameData\Bin\WASD.ini. The launcher has a new background and its version line is readable again, the Discord button links to our new server (discord.gg/hxFANB2kky), WASD movement now also works on Linux, and the two new cross chambers show a room picture in the Architect's Office instead of a wardrobe.

2026-11-21

Castle upgrades for level 60

Craft Goldmaw the Insatiable, Splendorado's golden dragon, for your own castle with the new Crafting Lab rank 15 (unlocks at castle heart 60; stronger than Dragonificus, twice the price). The Architect's Office goes to rank 7 (9 rooms, castle heart 40) and rank 8 (10 rooms, castle heart 50), and each rank unlocks two room types that were never for sale (Cross Chamber 04 and Linear Chamber 07, Cross Chamber 05 and Junction Chamber 04). Gold and Life Force Mines go to rank 13 (castle heart 38 and 52), and you get one more Gold, Life Force and Bling Mine at castle heart 35 and another at 55. Defense rating goes up to 750 at castle heart 60. Splendorado's player castles now have the bosses of the older regions in their throne rooms (Goldmaw only waits in Goldmaw's Hoard), the "35-60" badge no longer covers the region's dragon, and WASD movement now also works on Linux and Steam Deck.

2026-11-21

Level 60 fixes

Skill ranks III to V that had the same numbers now grow every rank (Hawk: Harass, Hawk: Dive, Stunning Blow, Siren Stun, Thunderclap, Teen Spirit, Headbang, Dancing Blades). Trap skills of all heroes: cooldown 10 seconds at rank I down to 7.5 seconds at rank V. Splendorado drops gear of the castle's level. Gold and Life Force Storage rank 6 (1,000,000) with castle heart 60. Goldmaw's golden dragon replaces the spider badge.

2026-11-21

Level 60 update

The level cap goes from 30 to 60. Every skill gets ranks IV and V, the castle heart goes to rank 60, and monsters, loot, the shop and the blacksmith go up to level 60. Beat Dragonificus to unlock the new region Splendorado (levels 35-60) under a golden sky, and defeat its golden dragon Goldmaw the Insatiable for the quest "All That Glitters". Monsters from level 35 are 22 % tougher. Hawk: Hack now has a 10-second cooldown.

Delivered automatically by the launcher: about 11 MB on the first start.

2026-11-21

WASD movement and key binds

Move your hero with W A S D during attacks while your mouse keeps aiming and attacking: whatever you pressed last wins, and your cursor never jumps. Ctrl+WASD walks without attacking, Shift+WASD holds the ground. The new KEYS button in the lobby top bar changes the WASD keys, the step distance and every game key bind.

Delivered automatically by the launcher and set up on the first start: the camera rotates with Q/E (and the arrow keys), Ability 5 moves to R. Change anything in the KEYS panel.

2026-11-20

"More castles" no longer freezes

Rerolling the castles on the attack map, with gold or with blings, could leave the game on an endless loading screen until you closed it. The server sent the new castle list with a message number the game had already seen, so the game ignored it and kept waiting. Fixed and tested in game: the new castles now show up every time.

Server-side fix, already live. Nothing to install. Gold and blings spent on rerolls were always charged correctly.

2026-11-20

Bling counter is back

The number of blings next to the green gem in the top bar was empty for every player: only the gem showed. The game files of the Steam version place the counter in a different spot than the script that fills it expects. The fix makes the script find the counter in both spots, and the amount now shows with thousands separators (1,230), also right after you earn or spend blings.

Delivered automatically: start the game from Steam and let the launcher finish its update. Your blings were never lost, only the display was empty.
