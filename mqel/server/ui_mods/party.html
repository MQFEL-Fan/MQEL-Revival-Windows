Co-op Raids

The Co-op Raids are procedural: the castles are generated for your party and are different from any story castle. The "More castles" button gives your whole party new ones.

Everyone in your party gets the same castles but plays them in their own game, with their own loot. Your friends' health, gold and loot show on the left during a raid.
