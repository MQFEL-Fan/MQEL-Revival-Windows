# 📜 Server Rules

Welcome, adventurer! This is a small community of players keeping an old game alive. Keep it friendly so everyone wants to stay.

**1. Respect everyone**
Treat other members the way you want to be treated. No insults, harassment, bullying, threats or personal attacks, in chat, in voice or in DMs to members of this server.

**2. No hate or discrimination**
No racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia or slurs, and no hate against anyone's religion, nationality or disability. Not even "as a joke".

**3. Keep it safe for work**
No NSFW, gore or shocking content, in messages, images, names, avatars or links.

**4. No spam**
No message floods, walls of emojis, mass pings, chain messages or unrelated self-promotion and advertising (including other Discord servers) without staff approval.

**5. Use the right channels**
Keep topics in their channels. Bug reports and game questions go where they belong, so staff can find them.

**6. Play fair**
No cheating, exploits, account sharing or selling, and no scams in trades or the Ingame Market. Found a bug or exploit? Report it to staff privately instead of abusing it or posting how it works.

**7. Protect your privacy, and everyone else's**
Never share anyone's personal information (real name, address, photos, IP, accounts) without their permission. Never ask for passwords or login codes. Staff will never ask for yours.

**8. Listen to staff**
Staff decisions are final in the moment. If you disagree, talk to a staff member calmly in private, not in public channels.

**9. Follow Discord's rules**
Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply here too.
・ Terms of Service: <https://discord.com/terms>
・ Community Guidelines: <https://discord.com/guidelines>

-# Breaking the rules can lead to a warning, a mute, a kick or a ban, depending on how serious it is.
